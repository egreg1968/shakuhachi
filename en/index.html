BEGINNERS

If you have never played

Most people arrive with the same worries. They are usually smaller than they look.

Is shakuhachi difficult?

The first job is simply making a sound. We go slowly. Students range from first-timers to people in their sixties and seventies.

Do I need my own instrument?

No. Handmade starter flutes are available from ¥5,000. Come and try before you buy.

What if I cannot make a sound?

That is normal. Air escapes at first. The class exists partly to share the moment it finally speaks.

Is there an age limit?

No upper limit. Current students include people aged 60–70. Younger students and complete beginners are especially welcome.

What music will I learn?

Breath and fingering first, then classical pieces and approachable tunes. We do not rush anyone into repertoire they are not ready for.

Are there performances?

Yes. Local festivals, competitions, and cultural exchanges. You can take part when you want to — it is an invitation, not a requirement.
